Features

Everything your week needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Kairo handles the busywork of scheduling so you can spend your energy on the work itself.

  • Auto-scheduling

    Add a task with a deadline and a time estimate. Kairo finds the best slot and books it on your calendar.

  • Protected focus time

    Set a weekly focus goal and Kairo defends it, holding long blocks free before meetings can claim them.

  • Smart rescheduling

    Meeting ran over? Urgent request? Your plan reshuffles in seconds, keeping deadlines and priorities intact.

  • Team availability

    See when everyone is actually free, across time zones, and book the slot that breaks the fewest focus blocks.

  • Workload insights

    A short weekly report shows where your time went, so you can spot meeting creep before it becomes a habit.

  • Two-way sync

    Connect the calendars and task tools you already use. Changes flow both ways in real time.

How it works

From chaos to a calm week in three steps

  1. 01

    Connect your calendar

    Sign in and link your work calendar. Kairo reads your existing meetings in under a minute.

  2. 02

    Add your priorities

    List what has to get done this week, how long each task takes and when it’s due.

  3. 03

    Let Kairo plan

    Get a realistic plan instantly. When things change, it adapts so you don’t have to.
